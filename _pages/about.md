My name is Cordelia Van der Veer, and I am a pre-doctoral fellow at the Yale Institution for Social and Policy Studies/Tobin Center for Economic Policy. I work with Professors Allison Harris and Dara Strolovitch on projects related to judicial politics and inequality. I graduated from The Ohio State University with a B.S. in Public Policy Analysis and Political Science. While at Ohio State, I won the John Glenn College of Public Affairs Award for Best Undergraduate Paper for my thesis, "Who Sues and Why: Environmental Nonprofits and Litigation." I also worked with Dr. Chris Rea (Brown University) as a research assistant and the Environmental History Initiative. I am a 2025 Beinecke Scholar and a 2025 Udall Scholar.
